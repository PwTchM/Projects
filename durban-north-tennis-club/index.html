About DNTC

A club built on community, sport & good tennis

Durban North Tennis Club is one of the area's premier community tennis facilities, welcoming players of every age and skill level. Whether you're picking up a racquet for the first time or chasing league points, there's a place for you on our courts.

Members enjoy quality courts, structured coaching, regular social sessions, competitive fixtures and dedicated pickleball facilities — plus a friendly clubhouse atmosphere that keeps people coming back.

  • Social & competitive tennis
  • Court & venue hire
  • Junior & adult coaching
  • Permanent pickleball courts
